    preview.changed

    "preview.changed": {
        confirmedVertices?: number;
        feature: FeatureInput | null;
        highlightVertex?: number;
    }

    The shape being drawn changed, or was cleared (feature is null). It fires once per change, with no throttling: at each vertex, at each move of the pointer that moves the shape, and when the shape is created, cancelled or left. feature has the type, the geometry and the layer of the shape, the ID it will be created with, and the radius of a circle; it is a copy that the drawing does not change afterwards. confirmedVertices and highlightVertex are the options the shape was shown with (ModeContext.preview.set): each is left out when it was not given, and both when the shape is cleared.
